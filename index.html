About

Technology works best when it works for people.

My path began with a Windows Phone app in 2011 and grew through native and cross-platform mobile development into technical leadership.

Today I initiate and deliver strategies that improve operational processes and team productivity. I lead software, quality assurance, and DevOps engineers through hiring, mentoring, coaching, one-to-ones, and performance development. My engineering background spans C#, Kotlin, Java, Objective-C, and Swift across Windows, Android, and iOS.

I earned an M.Sc. in Computer Science from Technische Universität Berlin, where my research focused on continuous integration in mobile development and automation for quality assurance teams.
